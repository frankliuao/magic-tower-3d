1. 引言与基本承诺

感谢您游玩由 Ao Liu（以下简称“开发者”或“我们”）开发的 3D 复古角色扮演策略游戏《魔塔 3D》（Magic Tower 3D）。我们极其重视并尊重每位登塔勇士的个人隐私权。

核心承诺：本应用是一款纯粹的单机复古策略游戏，我们绝不收集、不追踪、不出售、不向任何第三方共享您的任何个人身份信息。

2. 我们收集与处理的信息

《魔塔 3D》采用 100% 本地运行架构：

  • 无个人身份信息收集：我们不要求用户注册账号，不收集您的姓名、电话号码、电子邮箱、精确物理地理位置、通讯录、相册或麦克风等任何隐私数据。
  • 无设备唯一标识符追踪：应用不读取手机 IMEI、MAC 地址、Android ID 或广告标识符 (GAID)。
  • 本地游戏存档数据：为了保存您的游戏进度，游戏的关卡状态、角色生命值、攻击力、防御力、钥匙数量及存读档快照，仅保存在您当前设备本地的独立沙盒空间（LocalStorage）中，绝对不会上传至任何远程云端服务器。

3. 应用程序权限说明

本游戏遵循“最小权限原则”：

  • 网络权限（android.permission.INTERNET）：仅用于系统原生 WebView 组件加载本地 HTML5/Three.js 游戏渲染容器。游戏的所有 3D 几何建模、关卡数值和音效合成均由纯代码程序化本地生成，支持 100% 断网离线畅玩。
  • 震动反馈权限：仅在您移动、触碰机关或按下物理返回键时，调用系统触觉马达提供沉浸式轻微震动体验，不涉及任何数据记录。

4. 第三方服务与 SDK 政策

作为 Google Play 商店正式发布的纯净版本：

  • 本应用不包含任何第三方广告 SDK（无横幅广告、无弹窗广告、无开屏广告）；
  • 本应用不包含任何第三方追踪、归因或分析平台 SDK；
  • 本应用不包含任何外部支付与充值外链，完全符合 Google Play 开发者分发与安全政策。

1. Introduction & Core Commitment

Thank you for playing Magic Tower 3D, a 3D retro puzzle RPG developed by Ao Liu ("we", "us", or "developer"). We respect and value the privacy rights of all warriors ascending the tower.

Our Core Promise: Magic Tower 3D is a 100% standalone single-player game. We do NOT collect, track, sell, or share any personal identifying information with any third party.

2. Information Collection & Usage

Magic Tower 3D operates entirely on a local architecture:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not require accounts or logins. We never collect your name, phone number, email address, physical location, contacts, camera, or microphone data.
  • No Device Identifier Tracking: The application does not read hardware identifiers, IMEI, MAC address, Android ID, or Advertising ID (GAID).
  • Local Save Storage: To preserve your progress, game floor data, hero stats (HP, ATK, DEF, keys), and multi-slot snapshots are stored strictly in your device's local sandbox storage (LocalStorage). No save data is ever uploaded to remote cloud servers.

3. Permissions Used

The application complies with the principle of least privilege:

  • Internet Permission (android.permission.INTERNET): Used solely for the standard Android WebView container runtime. All 3D procedural geometries, dungeon layouts, and retro audio sound effects are synthesized locally on the fly—100% offline gameplay is fully supported.
  • Haptic Vibration: Invoked exclusively to deliver physical tactile feedback when walking, triggering traps, or pressing the back button.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

As an official Google Play release:

  • We do NOT embed any third-party advertisement SDKs (no banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads);
  • We do NOT integrate third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, or attribution libraries;
  • The app contains no external monetization links, strictly conforming to the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Magic Tower 3D is suitable for players of all ages, including children and families. We adhere strictly to COPPA and GDPR regulations:

  • We do not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or applicable local age threshold);
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete it.

6. Data Retention & User Rights

Because all game data resides strictly on your local device:

  • You maintain 100% full control over your game data;
  • You can overwrite or delete save slots at any time within the game's Save/Load menu;
  • You can clear app storage data via Android System Settings or uninstall the app to permanently remove all local records.
